ABOUT


    

      
GOBBONET

      
LOCAL AI TERMINAL // llama.cpp

      
A self-hosted chat workbench for any local GGUF model — characters, memory, branching, and deep sampler control, all running on hardware you own.

    


    


      

        
🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

        
Runs 100% on your machine — no accounts, no telemetry, no cloud round-trips. The only optional network call is the web-search proxy, and you decide when it's on. Your conversations and models never leave the building.

      


      

        
⚙ WHAT'S INSIDE

        

          
💬 Conversations — branch & fork any message into parallel timelines, reroll replies and step back through every variant, follow the model's reasoning in collapsible chain-of-thought, and schedule prompts to fire on their own.

          
🎭 Characters & memory — build character cards or import them from SillyTavern / TavernAI (V1–V3), swap personas per chat, fold older turns into compressed lore, and pull the right backstory in on cue with weighted-tag retrieval.

          
🎛 Fine control — per-character samplers (temperature, min-p, top-p, repeat penalty, XTC, DRY), logit bias to nudge or ban specific tokens, carousel system prompts, and {{macros}} that expand before they reach the model.

          
🧩 Extend & own it — inject your own CSS and JS, tune the interface right down to avatar size, reach it from any device on your LAN, and export or back up everything — threads, characters, settings — as portable JSON.

        

      


      

        
📚 STACK

        

          
Model — Local GGUF (detecting...)

          
Runtime — llama.cpp — runs any GGUF; CUDA/Vulkan/CPU

          
Frontend — Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS — no frameworks

          
Storage — IndexedDB in your browser — nothing server-side

          
Server — PowerShell HTTP listener + launch.bat
